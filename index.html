How it works

You pick a focus, the notebook hands you real goals, and everything you finish turns into something you can see.

01

Check off real quests

Daily and weekly goals from your focus, plus side quests you write yourself. Tick one off and watch the credits fly and your furniture get repaired.

02

Shop the morning paper

The Daily Nook arrives every morning with a few pieces for sale. Order what you love and it shows up in your room in a box.

03

Watch your room fill up

Place every piece wherever you like. Over the weeks your room becomes a record of the things you actually did.

A room that keeps time with you

  • Light follows the real time of day, and lamps and candles really light up the room.
  • A cat who lives there, hops on your furniture and knocks things off tables.
  • Collect every piece as a sticker, and hunt for rare finishes.
  • Thrift-store finds you restore back to their original beauty.
  • Missed a day? Nothing breaks. It just picks up where you left off.
